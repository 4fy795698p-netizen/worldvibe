Privacy

Public videos can be discovered by other users. Followers/private settings are stored with the post, but true media access control requires Firebase Storage security rules and/or a trusted backend.

Security

Use a strong password and never share your password. WorldVibe authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication.

WorldVibe Rules

Community Guidelines

Do not upload illegal, abusive, threatening, hateful, fraudulent or copyright-infringing content. Users can report content for moderation.

Privacy

WorldVibe stores account and content information needed to operate the service. Firebase services are used for authentication, database and media storage.

Terms

Withdraw
